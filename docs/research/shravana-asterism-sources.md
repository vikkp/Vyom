# Shravana: asterism research

## Decision

A three-star open line -- the existing yogatara Altair flanked by Tarazed
(γ Aquilae, to the north) and Alshain (β Aquilae, to the south), the real
"head of Aquila" triad. New `SHRAVANA_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "shravana"` with the existing yogatara. Same simple-line pattern
as Jyeshtha's three-star asterism.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Shravana consists of the stars Altair, Alshain and Tarazed,
  and these three stars together form the head of Aquila, the Eagle" --
  a well-documented, instantly recognisable naked-eye triad (Altair is the
  12th-brightest star in the sky, flanked by its two visibly fainter
  neighbours).
- **Symbol**: three footprints, tied to Vishnu's Vamana avatar, whose
  three strides measured the three worlds -- directly matching the
  three-star-in-a-row shape. "Shravana" itself means "hearing," and the
  ear is a secondary symbol.
  ([MyZodiaq](https://www.myzodiaq.in/en/online-library/27-nakshatras/shravana-nakshatra/shravana-nakshatra-symbol))
- **Ruling deity**: Vishnu, ruled by the Moon; also the birth nakshatra
  associated with Saraswati, goddess of learning.
  ([MyZodiaq](https://www.myzodiaq.in/en/online-library/27-nakshatras/shravana-nakshatra/shravana-nakshatra-symbol))

## Coordinates used

| Star | RA (h) | Dec (°) | Mag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Altair (existing yogatara) | 19.846 | 8.868 | 0.76 | unchanged from existing catalog entry |
| Tarazed (γ Aql) | 19.7710 | 10.6133 | 2.71 | [Wikipedia: Gamma Aquilae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_Aquilae) |
| Alshain (β Aql) | 19.9219 | 6.4068 | 3.87 | [Wikipedia: Beta Aquilae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_Aquilae) |

Tarazed sits north of Altair, Alshain sits south of it -- the three fall
in a near-straight line, matching the "three footprints" symbol directly.
Traversal path: Tarazed -> Altair -> Alshain.
