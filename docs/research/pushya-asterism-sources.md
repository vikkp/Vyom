# Pushya: asterism research

## Decision

A closed three-star triangle — same treatment as Mrigashira's "deer head"
(a closed loop back to the start, since three points read better as a
shape than an open line). The existing yogatara Asellus Australis (δ
Cancri, already `id: "asellus-australis"`, `nodeId: "pushya"`) is joined by
its traditional companions Asellus Borealis (γ Cancri) and Theta Cancri, in
a new `PUSHYA_EXTRA_STARS` group.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Pushya... corresponds to γ, δ and θ Cancri" — cross-checked
  across two independent sources (Rudraksha Ratna's astrology write-up and
  an aggregated summary citing the same three-star grouping), both naming
  the same trio rather than just the yogatara alone.
  ([Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/pushya-nakshatra))
  The three stars are also identified by their classical Western name, the
  "Little Asses" (Asellus Borealis/Australis flank the Praesepe/Beehive
  open cluster, M44, with Theta Cancri completing the group) — a
  double-confirmation that this specific trio (not some other nearby
  Cancer stars) is the traditional grouping.
- **Symbol**: cow's udder (the primary symbol — nourishment and
  life-sustaining abundance), with lotus and circular altar as secondary
  associations in some traditions; also arrow/circle in others. All the
  variants point at a compact, rounded shape rather than a long line — a
  closed triangle fits better than an open path.
  ([Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/pushya-nakshatra))
- **Ruling deity**: Brihaspati, guru/priest of the gods — matching Pushya's
  reputation as one of the most auspicious nakshatras for beginnings.
  ([Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/pushya-nakshatra))

## Coordinates used

| Star | RA (h) | Dec (°) | Mag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asellus Australis (δ Cnc, existing yogatara) | 8.745 | 18.154 | 3.94 | unchanged from existing catalog entry |
| Asellus Borealis (γ Cnc) | 8.7214 | 21.4686 | 4.65 | [Universe Guide](https://www.universeguide.com/star/42806/asellusborealis), cross-checked against [Wikipedia: Gamma Cancri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_Cancri) |
| Theta Cancri (θ Cnc) | 8.5266 | 18.0944 | 5.32 | [Wikipedia: Theta Cancri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_Cancri) |

Plotting RA as x / Dec as y: Theta and Asellus Australis sit at nearly the
same declination (~18.1°) with Theta to the west and Asellus Australis to
the east, while Asellus Borealis sits well north (~21.5°) roughly between
them in RA — a natural triangle/arrowhead shape, not a straight line.
Traversal path: Theta Cancri → Asellus Borealis → Asellus Australis →
Theta Cancri (closed).
