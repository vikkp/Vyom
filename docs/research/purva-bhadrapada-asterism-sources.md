# Purva Bhadrapada: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line -- the existing yogatara Markab (α Pegasi) joined by
Scheat (β Pegasi), the western pair of corners of the Great Square of
Pegasus. New `PURVA_BHADRAPADA_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "purva-bhadrapada"` with the existing yogatara. Same simple-line
pattern as Ashwini/Punarvasu.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Purva Bhadrapada consists of two bright stars, Merkab and
  Scheat. In modern astronomy, these stars are referred to as
  Alpha-Pegasi (Markab) and Beta-Pegasi (Scheat)" -- explicitly named,
  matching the existing yogatara plus one companion.
- **Symbol**: the front two legs of a funeral cot, a sword, and a
  two-faced man (one benign face, one violent) -- multiple related
  readings, all consistent with a simple two-point/two-legged shape.
- **Ruling deity**: Aja Ekapada, a one-footed serpent form of Rudra,
  representing unmanifest cosmic force. Ruled by Jupiter.
