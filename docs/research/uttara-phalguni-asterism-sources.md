# Uttara Phalguni: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line -- the existing yogatara Denebola (β Leonis) joined by 93
Leonis. New `UTTARA_PHALGUNI_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "uttara-phalguni"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Uttara Phalguni is formed by the stars Denebola
  (Beta-Leonis), and 93-Leonis. Denebola... is among the brightest stars
  in the night sky with a visual magnitude of 2.13, while 93-Leonis is a
  relatively faint star with a visual magnitude of 4.53" -- explicitly
  two named stars, matching the existing yogatara plus one companion.
- **Symbol**: the back legs of a bed/divan used for daytime lounging --
  continuing directly from Purva Phalguni's "front legs" of the same
  piece of furniture.
- **Ruling deity**: Aryaman, lord of philanthropy, hospitality, and the
  marriage covenant -- patronage and social dharma.
