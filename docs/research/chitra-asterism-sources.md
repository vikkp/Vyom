# Chitra: asterism research

## Decision

Chitra stays a **single-star** nakshatra, the same exception made for
Ardra, Swati, and Shatabhisha in earlier batches -- no new asterism entry,
no new star data. Multiple sources explicitly describe Chitra as "one of
the few asterisms which are represented by a single star," sometimes
called a "solitary star" in astrological tradition, corresponding to
Spica (α Virginis), which already exists in the catalog
(`id: "spica"`, `nodeId: "chitra"`). No traditionally sourced companion
stars turned up in research.

Applying the same resolution already confirmed for Ardra (stay
single-star rather than invent an unsourced companion grouping) without
re-asking, since it's the identical situation -- flagged here for
visibility.

The only new content from this pass is a placeholder figure (see
`MythicFigureOverlays.tsx`), anchored to the existing single star.

## Sourcing

- **Single-star confirmation**: "Chitra nakshatra is represented by a
  single star known as Spica (Alpha-Virginis) in the constellation of
  Virgo... Chitra is one of the few asterisms which are represented by a
  single star." No companion stars are cited anywhere in the sources
  checked.
- **Symbol**: a bright jewel or pearl, "sometimes depicted as a single
  luminous gem suspended on a cord" -- directly matching the single-point
  astronomical reality.
- **Ruling deity**: Tvashtar (Vishvakarma in later texts), the celestial
  architect and craftsman of the gods -- fitting Chitra's meaning,
  "brilliant" or "the bright one," and its reputation for skill and
  design.
