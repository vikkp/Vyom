# Swati: asterism research

## Decision

Swati stays a **single-star** nakshatra, the same exception made for Ardra
in the previous batch -- no new asterism entry, no new star data. Multiple
sources explicitly describe Swati as "one of the Nakshatras that is
represented by a single star," corresponding to Arcturus (α Boötis), which
already exists in the catalog (`id: "arcturus"`, `nodeId: "swati"`). No
traditionally sourced companion stars turned up in research, matching the
pattern that not every nakshatra beyond its yogatara has a documented
multi-star shape -- some (Punarvasu, Pushya, Ashlesha) genuinely do; others
(Ardra, Swati) genuinely don't.

Applying the same resolution the project owner already confirmed for Ardra
(keep it single-star rather than inventing an unsourced companion
grouping) without re-asking, since it's the identical situation -- flagged
here for visibility in case a different call is wanted for Swati
specifically.

The only new content from this pass is a placeholder figure (see
`MythicFigureOverlays.tsx`), anchored to the existing single star.

## Sourcing

- **Single-star confirmation**: "Swati Nakshatra... is one of the
  Nakshatra that is represented by a single star... this Nakshatra
  corresponds to the western star, Arcturus (Alpha-Bootis)." No companion
  stars are cited anywhere in the sources checked.
  ([MyZodiaq](https://www.myzodiaq.in/en/online-library/27-nakshatras/swati-nakshatra/swati-sprout-symbol))
- **Symbol**: a young plant shoot bent/blown by the wind (primary), with
  coral as an alternate symbol -- both convey delicacy and resilient
  flexibility rather than a multi-point shape.
  ([MyZodiaq](https://www.myzodiaq.in/en/online-library/27-nakshatras/swati-nakshatra/swati-sprout-symbol))
- **Ruling deity**: Vayu, the god of wind -- directly tied to the "shoot
  blown by the wind" symbolism. Ruled by Rahu.
  ([MyZodiaq](https://www.myzodiaq.in/en/online-library/27-nakshatras/swati-nakshatra/swati-sprout-symbol))
