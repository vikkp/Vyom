# Purva Phalguni: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line -- the existing yogatara Zosma (δ Leonis) joined by
Theta Leonis (Chertan). New `PURVA_PHALGUNI_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "purva-phalguni"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Purva Phalguni nakshatra consists of two bright stars in
  the night sky Zosma (Delta Leonis) and Chertan (Theta Leonis)... in the
  constellation of Leo, positioned to the left of the bright star
  Regulus" -- explicitly two named stars, matching the existing yogatara
  plus one companion.
- **Symbol**: the front legs of a bed, hammock, or divan/cot -- comfort,
  pleasure, and repose. A simple two-point shape (two front legs) matches
  a two-star line directly.
- **Ruling deity**: Bhaga, one of the twelve Adityas, associated with
  fortune, enjoyment, and the sharing of life's pleasures.
