# Purva Ashadha: asterism research

## Decision

A three-star open line -- the existing yogatara Kaus Media (δ Sagittarii)
joined by Kaus Borealis (λ Sagittarii, to the north) and Kaus Australis (ε
Sagittarii, to the south), the real "Kaus" trio marking the Archer's bow
in Sagittarius. New `PURVA_ASHADHA_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "purva-ashadha"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "The three stars Epsilon (Kaus Australis), Delta (Kaus
  Media), and Lambda (Kaus Borealis) form part of the Sagittarius
  constellation... Kaus Media marks the centre of the Archer's bow, while
  Kaus Australis marks the southern tip and Kaus Borealis marks the
  northern tip... Purva Ashada Nakshatra... consists of a group of three
  stars forming a shape of 'a hand held fan.'" Explicitly three named
  stars, matching the existing yogatara plus two companions, and their
  real positions form a real astronomical line (the visible bow of
  Sagittarius) independent of the nakshatra tradition.
- **Symbol**: an elephant's tusk, a hand-held fan, and a winnowing basket
  -- all consistent with an elongated, curved shape rather than a compact
  cluster, matching the real line the three stars form.
- **Ruling deity**: Apas (Apah), goddess of water and consort of Varuna.

Real declinations run cleanly north to south -- Kaus Borealis (-25.4,
northernmost) through Kaus Media (-29.8, centre) to Kaus Australis (-34.4,
southernmost) -- matching each star's own Arabic/Latin name ("Borealis" =
northern, "Australis" = southern). Traversal path: Kaus Borealis -> Kaus
Media -> Kaus Australis.
