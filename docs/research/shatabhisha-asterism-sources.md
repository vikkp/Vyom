# Shatabhisha: asterism research

## Decision

Shatabhisha stays a **single-star** nakshatra -- the same exception made
for Ardra and Swati in earlier batches -- no new asterism entry, no new
star data. Its classical symbol (an empty circle, "a hundred
physicians/stars") is deliberately about vastness and multiplicity rather
than a specific small shape, and research turned up no traditionally
documented small companion-star grouping, unlike Punarvasu, Pushya,
Ashlesha, or the rest of this batch (Purva/Uttara Bhadrapada, Revati),
which all have specific named companion stars.

Applying the same resolution already confirmed for Ardra (stay
single-star rather than invent an unsourced shape) without re-asking,
since it's the identical situation -- flagged here for visibility.

The only new content from this pass is a placeholder figure (see
`MythicFigureOverlays.tsx`), anchored to the existing single star, Lambda
Aquarii.

## Sourcing

- **Symbol**: "The primary symbol is an empty circle -- interpreted
  variously as a void, a hundred stars, or the unmanifest potential from
  which all healing emerges." Also called "Shatataraka" ("possessing a
  hundred stars"). "The asterism is... depicted [in temples] as a
  geometrically perfect formation of 100 small stars in circles and
  lines" -- an abstract, symbolic multiplicity, not a specific
  constellation shape.
  ([Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/shatabhisha-nakshatra))
- **Stars**: "The Shatabhisa asterism covers an area in Aquarius with
  countless faint stars." No specific secondary star (or small set of
  them) is consistently named as Shatabhisha's companion the way
  Punarvasu's Castor or Pushya's Asellus Borealis/Theta Cancri are --
  searches for a documented small grouping around the existing yogatara
  (Lambda Aquarii) turned up only its neighbouring *reference* star,
  Sadalmelik (Alpha Aquarii), used for locating it in the sky rather than
  as part of the nakshatra's own shape.
- **Ruling deity**: Varuna, lord of cosmic and terrestrial waters --
  fitting the "hundred healers" and the vast, oceanic scale of the "empty
  circle" symbolism.
