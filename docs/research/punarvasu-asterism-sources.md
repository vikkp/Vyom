# Punarvasu: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line asterism, the simplest possible shape (same pattern as
Ashwini's Sheratan–Mesarthim line): the existing yogatara Pollux (β
Geminorum, already in the catalog as `id: "pollux"`, `nodeId: "punarvasu"`)
joined to Castor (α Geminorum) — the two "twin" stars the nakshatra is
built on. Castor already existed in the catalog as an unlinked bright
naked-eye star (`BRIGHT_STARS`, no `nodeId`); it moves into a new
`PUNARVASU_EXTRA_STARS` group with `nodeId: "punarvasu"` and
`indianName: "Punarvasu"` set, same treatment every other multi-star
nakshatra's companion stars got.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Punarvasu Nakshatra... is made of the two brightest stars in
  the constellation of Gemini: Castor and Pollux" — both the yogatara
  (Pollux) and its twin (Castor) are explicitly named as the nakshatra's
  astronomical basis, not just Pollux alone.
  ([AstroVed](https://www.astroved.com/astropedia/en/nakshatra/punarvasu-nakshatra),
  [Sophia Astrology](https://www.sophiaastrology.com/post/punarvasu-nakshatra-souls-from-castor-and-pollux))
- **Symbol**: a bow and quiver of arrows — "the symbolic quiver of arrows
  depicts an unfaltering and steady progress towards ambitions in life
  against all odds," also tied to Rama's iconography.
  ([AstroVed](https://www.astroved.com/astropedia/en/nakshatra/punarvasu-nakshatra),
  [DK Score / Prash Trivedi](https://www.dkscore.com/jyotishmedium/punarvasu-nakshatra-by-prash-trivedi-297))
- **Ruling deity**: Aditi, mother of the Adityas and goddess of
  boundlessness/abundance — fitting for "Puna" (again) + "Vasu" (light/good),
  i.e. "the return of light" or "good again."
  ([AstroVed](https://www.astroved.com/astropedia/en/nakshatra/punarvasu-nakshatra))
- **Twin-star framing**: the two stars are consistently described as
  brothers with contrasting temperaments (Castor mischievous, Pollux
  generous/wise) — a real basis for a "twins" figure concept when real art
  is commissioned later.
  ([Sophia Astrology](https://www.sophiaastrology.com/post/punarvasu-nakshatra-souls-from-castor-and-pollux))
