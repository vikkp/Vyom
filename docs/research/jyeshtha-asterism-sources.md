# Research notes: Jyeshtha asterism

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

Third of the three-asterism batch (Ashwini, Magha, Jyeshtha) requested after ADR0003's original priority table. Stars + lines only.

## Star identification

Jyeshtha's existing yogatara is Antares (α Scorpii). The nakshatra-asterism is well-attested as a specific three-star group:

- "The Jyeshtha nakshatra is governed by three stars known as Alpha Scorpii (Antares), Tau Scorpii, and Sigma Scorpii... Three stars in a row, which were seen by ancients as forming a shape similar to an earring (or in some cases, the top of an umbrella)." [Grokipedia: Jyeshtha (nakshatra)](https://grokipedia.com/page/Jyeshtha_(nakshatra)), corroborated across multiple Vedic-astrology reference sources.

Coordinates (cross-checked against Wikipedia):

- **Sigma Scorpii** — RA 16h21m11.316s (16.3531h), Dec −25°35′34.05″ (−25.5928°), mag 2.88.
- **Tau Scorpii** — RA 16h35m52.953s (16.5981h), Dec −28°12′57.66″ (−28.2160°), mag 2.82.

Both share `nodeId: "jyeshtha"` with Antares.

## Shape

The three stars genuinely lie in a near-straight diagonal line by real position — Sigma (RA 16.35, Dec −25.6°) → Antares (RA 16.49, Dec −26.4°) → Tau (RA 16.60, Dec −28.2°), RA and Dec both increasing/decreasing monotonically — matching the "three stars in a row" / earring description directly, with no artistic reordering needed. Rendered as a simple open two-segment line, Sigma–Antares–Tau.
