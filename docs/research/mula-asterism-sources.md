# Mula: asterism research

## Decision

A nine-star open curved line -- the existing yogatara Shaula (λ Scorpii)
joined by Epsilon, Mu¹, Zeta², Eta, Theta (Sargas), Iota¹, Kappa (Girtab),
and Upsilon (Lesath) Scorpii, the full curved "scorpion's tail / lion's
tail" asterism. New `MULA_EXTRA_STARS` group, all sharing
`nodeId: "mula"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Mula consists of a group of nine stars... forming the shape
  of a 'Lion's tail.' In modern astronomy, the nine stars are... Lambda
  (Shaula), Epsilon, Mu-1, Iota-1, Theta, Eta, Zeta, Kappa and Upsilon
  (Lesath) Scorpionis." Nine stars named explicitly, matching the existing
  yogatara plus eight companions.
  ([AnytimeAstro](https://www.anytimeastro.com/blog/nakshatra/moola-nakshatra/))
  This is the same real asterism Western astronomy calls the "Scorpion's
  tail" or "fish-hook" -- a well-documented, easily recognised naked-eye
  curve, cross-checked against each star's individual Wikipedia entry.
- **Symbol**: a tied bunch of roots, signifying digging down to the very
  foundation/root of existence -- "Mula" literally means "the Root."
  ([Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/moola-nakshatra))
- **Ruling deity**: Nirriti, goddess of dissolution and destruction who
  clears away decayed structures for renewal -- fitting the "root/core"
  and Ketu-ruled themes. ([Rudraksha
  Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/moola-nakshatra))

Plotting RA as x / Dec as y, the real positions trace the actual
"fish-hook" curve: starting near the scorpion's body (Epsilon, least
negative Dec), sweeping south through Mu¹, Zeta², Eta (the southernmost
point), then curving back north-east through Theta, Iota¹, Kappa, to the
stinger tip pair Upsilon/Shaula (Lesath and Shaula are the classic "Cat's
Eyes," only ~0.2° apart). Traversal path (open, not closed -- a tail has
two ends, not a loop): Epsilon -> Mu¹ -> Zeta² -> Eta -> Theta -> Iota¹ ->
Kappa -> Upsilon -> Shaula.
