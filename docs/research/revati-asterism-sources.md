# Revati: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line -- the existing yogatara Zeta Piscium joined by Mu
Piscium, its documented near neighbour at the end of the western fish's
tail in Pisces. New `REVATI_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "revati"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "Zeta Piscium is located close to a comparatively brighter
  star, Mu Piscium, around the end of the tail of fish formed by
  constellation Meena [Pisces]." A specific, named companion star,
  distinguishing Revati from Shatabhisha (which has no such documented
  companion in this batch). Angular separation is about 4.3 degrees --
  close enough to read as a real pair rather than a coincidental
  neighbour. ([Sophia Astrology](https://www.sophiaastrology.com/post/revati-nakshatra-souls-from-zeta-piscium))
- **Symbol**: a fish swimming in the sea, and a drum ("Dhola," a
  two-headed hand-drum with strings across the neck) -- both point at an
  elongated shape, consistent with a simple two-star line rather than a
  compact cluster.
- **Ruling deity**: Pushan, solar deity of safe travel and guidance --
  fitting Revati's role as the final nakshatra, marking completion of the
  journey through all 27.
