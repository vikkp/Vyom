# Uttara Bhadrapada: asterism research

## Decision

A two-star line -- the existing yogatara Algenib (γ Pegasi) joined by
Alpheratz (α Andromedae), the eastern pair of corners of the Great Square
of Pegasus (together with Purva Bhadrapada's Markab/Scheat pair, the full
Great Square is split across these two adjacent nakshatras -- a real,
sourced astronomical fact, not a coincidence of naming). New
`UTTARA_BHADRAPADA_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "uttara-bhadrapada"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "In modern astronomy, these stars are referred to as
  Gamma-Pegasi (Algenib) and Alpha-Andromedae (Alpheratz). It consists of
  two bright stars that form a figure of a 'rectangular bed.'" Explicitly
  named, matching the existing yogatara plus one companion.
- **Symbol**: the back two legs of a funeral cot/sleeping bed, twins, and
  a serpent in water ("a serpent with two and a half coils," a Kundalini
  image) -- again a simple two-point/two-legged reading.
- **Ruling deity**: Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep waters --
  hidden wisdom and the subconscious.
