# Anuradha: asterism research

## Decision

A three-star open line -- Beta Scorpii (Graffias/Acrab, to the north)
joined to the existing yogatara Dschubba (δ Scorpii, centre) and Pi
Scorpii (to the south). New `ANURADHA_EXTRA_STARS` group, sharing
`nodeId: "anuradha"` with the existing yogatara.

## Sourcing

- **Stars**: "It is the group of three stars forming the shape of 'a
  staff'. The names of three stars in Western astronomy are - Beta
  Scorpionis, Delta Scorpionis (Isidis) and Pi-Scorpionis. These stars
  align in a nearly straight line above the brighter red supergiant
  Antares" -- explicitly three named stars in a stated straight-line
  shape, matching the existing yogatara plus two companions.
- **Symbol**: a staff, and separately a lotus and "a triumphant gateway
  adorned with leaves" -- the staff reading matches the real straight-line
  shape most directly.
- **Ruling deity**: Mitra, one of the twelve Adityas, god of friendship,
  good faith, and warmth.

## Coordinates used

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dschubba / δ Scorpii (existing yogatara) | 16.006 | -22.622 | 2.32 | unchanged from existing catalog entry |
| Beta Scorpii (Graffias / Acrab) | 16.0906 | -19.8055 | 2.62 | [Wikipedia: Beta Scorpii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_Scorpii) |
| Pi Scorpii | 15.9808 | -26.1141 | 2.89 | [Wikipedia: Pi Scorpii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_Scorpii) |

Declinations run cleanly north to south -- Beta Scorpii (-19.8) through
Dschubba (-22.6) to Pi Scorpii (-26.1, just north of Antares at -26.4,
matching the "align... above Antares" description). Traversal path:
Beta Scorpii -> Dschubba -> Pi Scorpii.
